# Provenance of the preserved alternatives

The files K1_proof_note.md and section17.4.3-baseline.tex come unchanged from the 24 November manuscript materials. They preserve the 24 November approach to the uniform cohomological order bound and the qualifications in the original note. Section 17.4.3 of the current PDF develops the cyclotomic, lattice and homology proof in the pinned release.

The file terminal-exponent.tex preserves the previous error proposition with the maximum of the exponents. At (2,0) it gives a prime-sum loss of seven epsilon. The live original deduction uses the sum of the exponents and loses nine epsilon. Both suffice for the limiting half-plane; their derivations should be evaluated separately.
